### Ethiopia HIV IG (sandbox demo subset)

Minimal HIV IG for the conformance sandbox. Depends on [`et.fhir.surveillance`](http://fhir.et/surveillance)
(and transitively [`et.fhir.core`](http://fhir.et/core)). Enables `HIVPatient` (requires an ART
number), `HIVEncounter`, and `HIVMedicationDispense` for the medication-dispense scenario; the rest of
the HIV content ships disabled.
